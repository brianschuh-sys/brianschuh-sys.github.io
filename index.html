Coach's Tips
  • Imagine you're one of the defenders — follow him with your eyes as the ball and offense move around.
  • Every defense has vulnerabilities. Tell Coach if you figure them out.
  • Think about what qualities each defense needs from its players to succeed — is anything universal across all of them?
